02 — Understand the hierarchy

Six levels. One clear structure.

HTML provides six heading levels. H1 represents the main topic, while H2 to H6 organize increasingly specific sections.

Heading hierarchy

H1 Main page title Largest
H2 Major section Level 2
H3 Subsection title Level 3
H4 Smaller heading Level 4
H5 Minor heading Level 5
H6 Smallest heading Smallest
RULE / 01

Use H1 for the main topic

It introduces the primary subject of the page. Keep your heading hierarchy meaningful.

RULE / 02

Don't skip levels randomly

Use headings in a logical order so readers and assistive technologies can understand the structure.

RULE / 03

Paragraphs explain ideas

Use the paragraph tag to group related sentences into readable blocks of text.
